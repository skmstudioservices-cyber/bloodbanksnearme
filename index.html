🚨 In an emergency — the 3 numbers to know

  • 108 — free ambulance (public-private service across most states)
  • 112 — national emergency number (police, fire, medical)
  • 1910 — blood donation helpline operating in several states

Save these in your phone now. When you call, state the city, hospital and blood group needed immediately.

🩸 Find a verified blood bank — e-RaktKosh

>

e-RaktKosh (eraktkosh.in) is the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare's national blood bank network. It lists registered blood banks across India with location, contact details and blood stock availability — the most reliable way to check who has what, before you travel.

Steps: open eraktkosh.in → "Blood Bank Directory" → select your state and district → see registered banks and their contact numbers. Numbers there are government-registered.

Why we don't publish phone numbers directly: outdated or wrong blood bank numbers can cost lives. We are building a call-verified directory — until every number is verified, we point only to official sources.

🩸 Blood donation — quick eligibility basics

>
  • Age 18–65, weight above 50 kg, normal temperature and pulse
  • Haemoglobin ≥ 12.5 g/dL; not donated in the last 3 months (men)
  • No alcohol in the last 24 hours; eat and hydrate before donating
  • Not pregnant or breastfeeding; defer if unwell or on certain medications

One donation can save up to three lives. In stable times, donate at hospitals or licensed camps — your blood goes into the e-RaktKosh network.

🧭 While waiting for the ambulance

>

Keep the patient calm and lying down. If a doctor has advised it, keep any existing prescription or blood group card ready. Don't give food or water if surgery is possible. Have one person keep calling nearby hospitals' blood banks (from e-RaktKosh) with the blood group and units needed, while someone else handles the ambulance.

❓ Frequently asked questions

How do I find a blood bank near me in an emergency?

Call 108 or 112 immediately, and check eraktkosh.in — the government's directory of registered blood banks with live stock information. District and government hospitals also have attached blood banks.

What is the blood helpline number in India?

1910 is the blood donation helpline in several states. For emergencies, 108 (ambulance) and 112 (national emergency) are the primary numbers.

What is e-RaktKosh?

e-RaktKosh is the Ministry of Health's national platform connecting registered blood banks across India, with a searchable directory and blood availability information.

Who can donate blood in India?

Generally: age 18–65, weight over 50 kg, haemoglobin ≥ 12.5 g/dL, no donation in the past 3 months, and in normal health. Final eligibility is decided at the centre.
